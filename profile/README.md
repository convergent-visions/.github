
# Convergent Visions

*An open research collective where collaboration comes before credit*

---

We believe there is one science.

We believe science advances through collaboration, not competition.

No single discipline has all the answers. No single laboratory has to own a question. The most interesting discoveries often happen where fields, ideas, and people meet.

We want to build a community where researchers share knowledge, methods, code, and ideas — and where collaboration matters more than claiming territory.

Different disciplines. Different perspectives. One shared vision.

**If your vision converges with ours, join us.**
